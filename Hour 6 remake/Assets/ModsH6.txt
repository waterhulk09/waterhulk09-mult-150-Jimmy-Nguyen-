A. Add multiple finish zones. See if you can place them in a way that offers the player more choices. For example, a dangerous shortcut route vs an easy but lengthier one. I Added 2 paths one hard and one easy the hard path has is shorter while the easy path is a longer. They both point to the same end goal.



B. Modify the level to have more, and different hazards. (At least 2 new hazards that are not water.) I have added spikes and a laser pits (some people would call them lava jumps) 



C. Modify the game to include an element of verticality in your game to make use of the jumping mechanic! (spacebar to jump) the whole game is a vertical jumping platformer that make use of the jumping mechanic 



D. Modify the sky and textures to create a world based on your theme. I uses sprites, models, sounds and music from a sonic game. (there was 2 that was unrelated that being the spikes and the I beam however the rest where) The sound effects I used came from various games that being Sonic 1 for the jump, Sonic Mania for the falling and Sonic unleashed for the footsteps.
